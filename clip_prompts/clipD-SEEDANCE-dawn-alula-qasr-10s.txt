★ THE LANDMARKS ARE SOLID PHYSICAL OBJECTS AND NEVER TRANSITION. THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE IN THE SHOT.

Each rock formation is a real, solid, opaque mass of stone that exists in the world for the entire 10 seconds, whether the camera can see it or not. It is not an effect. It does not arrive. It does not appear. It is simply already there, and the camera flies to where it can be seen.

THE VERY FIRST INSTANT ANY PART OF A ROCK IS VISIBLE, THAT PART IS ALREADY 100% SOLID — full opacity, full contrast, full colour saturation, full sharpness, full detail, full weight. It NEVER begins faint, pale, soft, dim, hazy, washed out, low-contrast, ghostly, translucent, semi-transparent or partly formed and then strengthen, darken, sharpen, solidify or fill in. There is NO opacity change on the stone at any point. NO alpha blending, NO cross-fade, NO opacity ramp, NO double exposure, NO ghosting, NO shimmer-in, NO particles assembling, NO growing out of the vapour, NO condensing, NO materialising, NO emerging into existence.

The ONLY way more of a rock becomes visible is that vapour which was physically in front of it moves out of the way, or the camera changes position so that less vapour lies between. This is pure physical occlusion — one solid object hidden behind another, exactly like a mountain becoming visible as fog clears in front of it. Rock that is uncovered is uncovered instantly and completely, edge by edge, as a hard solid silhouette against the sky.

PACING. The viewpoint moves at ONE MODERATE, EVEN, HUMAN-PACED FLYING SPEED for the entire 10 seconds — the unhurried pace of a real pilot cruising, not a racing machine. It is NEVER fast. It NEVER accelerates hard, NEVER surges, NEVER rushes, NEVER whips, NEVER blurs with speed. Equally it NEVER STOPS, NEVER FREEZES, NEVER HOVERS MOTIONLESS, NEVER PARKS on a landmark. There is always gentle forward drift and gentle rotation — the camera is alive and breathing at every single frame. No speed ramping, no slow motion, no fast motion, no time remapping. At the rock formation the viewpoint merely eases a little and glides slowly past while still moving; it does not halt to pose.

THE TRANSITION RULE — THE CLOUD IS THE ONLY TRANSITION IN THIS SHOT. Both rock formations stand above an unbroken deck of cloud. To leave a formation, the viewpoint banks over and DESCENDS DOWN INTO THAT CLOUD DECK, sinking physically into the vapour below it. The cloud tops rise up past the lens from the bottom of frame as the camera goes down into them, wisps whipping upward past the frame edges as it sinks, until the viewpoint is completely inside the cloud and vapour fills the frame edge to edge. It then travels forward through the inside of the cloud, and climbs gently back up and out of the cloud top on the far side. The viewpoint must be SEEN to physically enter the cloud from above and SEEN to physically climb out of it. All change of place happens inside the vapour, where nothing can be seen. Nothing transitional ever happens to a landmark itself.

THE ROCK IS NEVER THE DOORWAY. The viewpoint NEVER flies into the rock, NEVER flies at the rock, NEVER flies through the rock, NEVER passes through the arch opening, NEVER enters any hole, cave, crack or gap in the stone. It keeps its distance from the stone at all times and only ever passes it at a distance. ONLY CLOUD IS EVER ENTERED.
