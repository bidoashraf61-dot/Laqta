THIS IS A PURE DISEMBODIED FIRST-PERSON VIEWPOINT — the camera is the viewer's own eyes flying freely through the air. The frame contains ONLY sky, cloud and landscape. PACING — THE MOST IMPORTANT INSTRUCTION. The viewpoint moves at ONE MODERATE, EVEN, HUMAN-PACED FLYING SPEED for the entire shot — the unhurried pace of a real pilot cruising, not a racing machine. The cloud tops rise up past the lens from the bottom of frame as the camera goes down into them, wisps whipping up the sides, until the viewpoint is completely inside the cloud and vapour fills the frame edge to edge. It then travels forward through the inside of the cloud, and climbs gently back up and out of the cloud top on the far side to find the next place. The viewpoint must be SEEN to physically enter the cloud from above and SEEN to physically leave it from inside. It never simply meets a wall of fog, never has cloud slide across the lens, never has the image turn white or grey and reappear elsewhere. The descent into the cloud and the climb out of it are real flight moves the viewer watches happen.
