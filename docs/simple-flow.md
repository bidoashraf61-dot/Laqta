# The Portal — Simple Flow

Business registered in **Egypt**. Sells online. Buyers mainly Saudi. Creators mainly Egyptian.

---

## PART 1 — How the business works (the money flow)

```
Creator uploads an album  →  You review & approve  →  Album goes live
        ↓
Buyer finds a clip  →  sees it belongs to an album  →  buys the album (one-time)
        ↓
Buyer pays online (card / Fawry / wallet)
        ↓
Money goes to YOUR account
        ↓
You keep your commission (e.g. 30%)  →  Creator gets the rest (70%)
        ↓
Buyer downloads the album forever
```

That's the whole model. You are the shop. Creators supply. You take a cut.

---

## PART 2 — What you need to set up (business side)

1. **Register the company in Egypt** — commercial register + tax card (tax number).
2. **Get a payment gateway** — Paymob, Fawry, or Kashier (accepts local + international cards).
3. **Set up e-invoicing** — Egyptian ETA system (your accountant handles this).
4. **Open a business bank account** — to receive sales money.
5. **Set up creator payouts** — local transfer/Instapay for Egyptians, Payoneer/Wise for others.
6. **Get 3 documents written** (by a lawyer):
   - Buyer licence (what buyers are allowed to do with the footage)
   - Creator agreement (commission %, payout rules)
   - Terms + privacy + refund policy

---

## PART 3 — What you need to build (the website)

Build it in this order:

### Step 1 — The catalogue
- Upload system for videos (large files)
- Auto-create watermarked previews (buyers see low-quality previews only)
- Store the original hi-res file safely (only buyers get it)

### Step 2 — The buyer side
- **Search & browse** clips (with filters: location, 4K, drone, vertical, etc.)
- **Clip page** — shows the clip + "this clip is part of Album X — SAR ___"
- **Album page** — all clips in the album + price + Buy button
- **Cart & checkout** — pay by card / Fawry / wallet
- **My Library** — buyer downloads their albums, forever

### Step 3 — The creator side
- **Sign up as a creator** + apply
- **Upload an album** (title, clips, price, permits)
- **Earnings dashboard** — see sales + request payout

### Step 4 — Your admin side
- **Review queue** — approve or reject each album before it goes live
- **Orders & payouts** — see sales, pay creators
- **Homepage control** — feature the best albums

---

## PART 4 — The launch plan (simple)

1. **Set up the company + payment gateway** (do this first, it takes time).
2. **Build the website** (catalogue → buyer side → creator side → admin).
3. **Add your own 3–5 albums** so the site isn't empty.
4. **Invite 2–4 trusted Egyptian creators** to add their albums.
5. **Soft launch** — test with a few real buyers.
6. **Public launch** — open creator sign-ups + market it.

---

## The golden rules (don't break these)

- **People search for clips, but buy albums.** Every clip must point to its album + price.
- **Never launch empty.** Have real albums ready before opening.
- **Approve every album before it's live.** Quality + permits protect you legally.
- **Buyer owns what they bought forever** — even if the creator later removes the album.
- **Freeze the commission at sale time** — if you change your % later, old sales stay as they were.
