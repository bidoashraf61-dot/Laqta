Album rules — the quality moat

  • One subject. "AlUla — Golden Hour Aerials" ✓ · "Best of Saudi" ✗
  • One look. Same grade, same spec, same frame rate — it must cut together untouched
  • Minimum 15 clips, cover clip and stills chosen deliberately
  • Both orientations delivered — vertical produced by reframing the 4K master, never regenerated

Album coherence is the single biggest lever on refund rate and repeat purchase. A dumping ground is not an album.

05 — The licence

One licence. Forever.

This is the argument that wins agencies. Price wins freelancers; the licence wins the people with budgets.

Included

  • Social, YouTube, web, apps
  • Paid advertising — no impression cap
  • Client work — unlimited clients
  • Television broadcast
  • Cinema and screenings
  • Billboards, OOH, outdoor screens
  • Events and exhibitions
  • Editing, cutting, compositing
  • Up to 10 people in the company
  • Worldwide · Perpetual

Excluded

  • Reselling the clips as stock
  • Training AI models
  • Editorial or news use — the content is generated and depicts nothing real
  • Implying endorsement by a real person or body
  • Anything unlawful under Saudi regulation

10 — Arabic register

Arabic register

Saudi. One register everywhere, no exceptions.

Saudi colloquial with an MSA backbone — colloquial rhythm and vocabulary, full sentences, correct grammar. Not street slang, not corporate Arabic. The test: would a Riyadh creative director say this out loud in a meeting?

✓ We use

تقدرnot يمكنك
الحينnot حالياً
عندكnot لديك
ما فيهnot لا يوجد
شغلةnot شغلانة

✗ We never use

Egyptian: شغلانة · دلوقتي · عايز · كده · إزاي

Corporate: نسعى · نفخر · رائدة · حلول متكاملة

Hedging: ربما · قد يكون · تقريباً

Empty: جودة عالية · أسعار تنافسية

Never mix dialects on one pageAn early draft ran Gulf مو next to Egyptian شغلانة. That reads as two different writers, and for a brand asking agencies to trust it with a broadcast licence, that costs real credibility.

Known trade-off

Standard guidance for a multi-regional Arab audience is MSA with light dialect. Saudi register was chosen deliberately: it buys credibility with the buyers who hold the budgets, and Egyptian readers understand Gulf dialect from media exposure. Accepted, not accidental.

The ground ladder

Sections don't sit on one canvas. A band opts into a rung — never by setting a colour directly. Light body, black frame, olive on exactly three surfaces: the collection band, the creator CTA, and the footer.

The letterbox is the recurring geometry: the shape of the product itself. Every thumbnail, hero and empty state is a dark frame with paper around it.

13 — The named rules

The named rules

The One Voice RuleGold means money, the primary action, or the active state — nothing else. Under 15% of any screen. Audit test: if two gold things compete for the eye, one is wrong.
The Dark-Is-A-Frame Rule.dark describes footage, never chrome. The document is paper. Premium comes from the frames being the only dark things on screen.
The Isolated-Latin RuleAny Latin run inside Arabic — a codec, a price, an order number — is wrapped .ltr-island or .numeric. Un-isolated, "Rec.709" renders as "709.Rec".

14 — Content & ethics

Content & ethics

1 · AI is disclosed, alwaysEvery album carries a visible badge. It appears on the homepage, in the FAQ, on every album page and in the licence. We never imply the footage is filmed. Being caught concealing it would end the business — and disclosed well, it is the reason the price exists.
2 · No religious sites, everNo Makkah, no Madinah, no mosque interiors. AI depictions of the Two Holy Mosques are religiously and legally sensitive in Saudi Arabia. Not generated, not sold, not shown.
3 · No editorial or news useThe footage depicts nothing real. It cannot be used in journalism, documentary, or any context implying a real event, place or person. Stated in the licence.
4 · No identifiable real peopleNo real individuals, no implied endorsement.
5 · Saudi content standardsModest dress, appropriate gender interaction, no alcohol. Cultural QC runs on every shot involving people before it is published. This is not optional.
6 · No competitor is ever named publiclyComparisons stay anonymous. Real figures are internal only, and every published number must be provable with a dated screenshot.
